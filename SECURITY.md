## 🛰️ Reporting a Vulnerability

We take security seriously and appreciate the efforts of the research community to keep our users safe. If you discover a vulnerability, please follow the protocol below:

### 📥 Submission Process
Do **not** open a public issue. Instead, please report vulnerabilities via one of the following channels:
1.  **Email**: Send a detailed report to [security@freelance-pay.cloud](mailto:harshitmehta1012@gmail.com).
2.  **Encrypted Message**: Use the internal Admin Command Center if you have root access.

### ⏱️ Response Timeline
- **Initial Acknowledgement**: Within 24 hours.
- **Detailed Evaluation**: Within 3 business days.
- **Status Updates**: Every 48 hours until the vulnerability is patched.

### 🛡️ Bug Bounty Policy
We operate a **Responsible Disclosure** policy. If the vulnerability is verified and has a significant impact, we will:
- Publicly credit you in our `CREDITS.md`.
- Provide early access to our experimental "Elite Tier" features.
- Provide a certificate of appreciation.
